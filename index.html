Common questions

Do you really come to my house?

Yes. We are a mobile service, so every installation, repair and service is done at your home or business.

How do I get a quote?

Call or WhatsApp 083 985 3567 with your suburb and what you need. For repairs, a photo of the unit and any error code on the display helps us quote faster.

Which brands do you work on?

All major brands, including Samsung, LG and Jet-Air, as well as other split and inverter systems.

How often should an aircon be serviced?

Most units should be cleaned and checked at least once a year. Units that run all day, like in offices and shops, benefit from a service every six months.
