Common questions

Do you really come to my house?

Yes. We are a mobile service, so every installation, repair and service is done at your home or business.

Do you offer emergency call outs?

Yes. We offer 24/7 emergency support. Call or WhatsApp 083 985 3567 any time, day or night.

How do I get a quote?

Call or WhatsApp 083 985 3567 with your suburb and what you need. For repairs, a photo of the unit and any error code on the display helps us quote faster.

Which brands do you work on?

All major brands, including Samsung, LG and Jet-Air. We install and service midwall split, cassette, ducted and floor standing systems.

Which type of aircon is right for me?

It depends on the space. Midwall splits suit homes and apartments, cassettes suit offices and shops, ducted systems suit larger buildings and floor standing units suit industrial sites. We give professional advice before every installation, based on your room size, layout, usage and budget.

How often should an aircon be serviced?

Most units should be cleaned and checked at least once a year. Units that run all day, like in offices and shops, benefit from a service every six months.
