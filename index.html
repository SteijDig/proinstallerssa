Installation options

Which aircon suits your space?

Every building cools differently. We install four main types of system, so you get one that fits the space and the budget instead of whatever is in stock.

  • Midwall split

    Homes and apartments

    The everyday choice for bedrooms, lounges and small offices. A quiet unit on the wall inside, the compressor outside.

  • Cassette

    Offices and retail spaces

    Sits flush in the ceiling and blows air four ways, so open floors cool evenly with nothing on the walls.

  • Ducted

    Larger homes and buildings

    One hidden unit feeds several rooms through slim ceiling vents. Whole building comfort with a clean finish.

  • Floor standing

    Industrial and large spaces

    High output units that move a lot of air in big, high-ceiling spaces like workshops, halls and warehouses.

Not sure which one?

Send us the room size and a photo. We will recommend a system and size based on the layout, how you use the space and your budget.

Ask for advice

Worth asking about: inverter units

Inverter models speed up and slow down instead of switching on and off. They use less electricity, run quieter and are easier on solar and backup power systems.

Yes. We are a mobile team, so every installation, repair and service happens at your home or business, anywhere in Gauteng.

How do I get a quote?

Use the quick quote above, or call or WhatsApp 083 985 3567 with your suburb and what you need. For repairs, a photo of the unit and any error code on the display helps us quote faster.

Which brands do you work on?

All major brands, including Samsung, LG and Jet-Air. We install and service midwall split, cassette, ducted and floor standing systems.

Which type of aircon is right for me?

It depends on the space. Midwall splits suit homes and apartments, cassettes suit offices and shops, ducted systems suit larger buildings and floor standing units suit industrial sites. We give advice before every installation, based on room size, layout, usage and budget.

How often should my aircon be serviced?

At least once a year for most homes. Units that run all day, like in offices and shops, do better with a service every six months. Before summer is the best time.

My aircon is dripping water. Is that serious?

Usually it is a blocked drain or a dirty coil, which a service sorts out. Switch the unit off if water is running down the wall and send us a photo.
